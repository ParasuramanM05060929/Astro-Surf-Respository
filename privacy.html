Privacy Policy

App Name: Astro Surf
Developer: Parasu Pradhee PPTS Developer
Effective Date: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Astro Surf ("the App", "we", "our") handles user information. We respect your privacy and are committed to providing a safe, offline, and ad-free gaming experience.

1. Information Collection and Use

Astro Surf does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or sensitive user data.

  • We do not require account registration, login, name, email address, phone number, or location access.
  • The game runs completely offline and does not send any gameplay or device data to external servers.

2. Local Game Data Storage

To save your game progress (such as unlocked levels, high scores, and sound/control preferences), Astro Surf uses standard local storage directly on your Android device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us or any third party.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Astro Surf is completely ad-free and does not integrate any third-party advertising networks, analytics tools, or tracking SDKs.

4. Data Security, Retention, and Deletion

Because we do not collect or store any user data on external servers, there is no remote data to retain or breach. All locally stored game progress remains strictly on your device until you choose to delete it. You can permanently delete all local game data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the App.

5. Children's Privacy

Astro Surf is safe for players of all ages. Since the App does not collect any personal information from anyone, it fully complies with children's privacy standards, including COPPA and Google Play Families policies.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if future versions of Astro Surf introduce new features. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
